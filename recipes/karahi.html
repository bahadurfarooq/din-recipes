Biryani Recipe

Description:

Chicken Karahi is one of the most popular dishes in Karachi. It is cooked in a wok (karahi) with fresh tomatoes, ginger, garlic, and green chilies. It has a spicy, rich, and slightly tangy taste, usually served with naan.

Ingredients:

  • Chicken
  • Tomatoes
  • Ginger-garlic paste
  • Green chilies
  • Red chili powder
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Cooking oil
  • Fresh coriander
  • Ginger (sliced for garnish)

Steps:

  1. Heat oil in a karahi and add chicken.
  2. Cook until chicken changes color.
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste and tomatoes.
  4. Cook until tomatoes become soft.
  5. Add spices and mix well.
  6. Cook on high flame until oil separates.
  7. Garnish with green chilies, ginger, and coriander.
  8. Serve hot with naan.

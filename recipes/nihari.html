Nihari Recipe

Description:

Nihari is a traditional slow-cooked meat stew, very famous in Karachi for breakfast. It is made with beef or mutton and cooked overnight with spices for a deep, rich flavor.

Ingredients:

  • Beef or mutton
  • Nihari masala
  • Wheat flour (for thickening)
  • Ginger-garlic paste
  • Red chili powder
  • Salt
  • Cooking oil or ghee
  • Fried onions
  • Lemon
  • Green chilies

Steps:

  1. Cook meat with spices and oil on low heat.
  2. Add water and simmer for several hours.
  3. Mix wheat flour in water and add to thicken gravy.
  4. Cook until meat becomes soft.
  5. Serve with fried onions, lemon, and green chilies.

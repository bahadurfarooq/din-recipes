Biryani Recipe

Description:

Karachi-style Beef Biryani is a spicy and aromatic rice dish popular in Pakistan, especially in Karachi. It is made with tender beef cooked in rich masala and layered with fragrant basmati rice. The dish is known for its bold flavors, spicy kick, and street-style taste.

Ingredients:

  • Beef (bone-in or boneless)
  • Basmati rice
  • Yogurt
  • Onions (sliced)
  • Tomatoes
  • Ginger-garlic paste
  • Green chilies
  • Biryani masala
  • Red chili powder
  • Turmeric powder
  • Salt
  • Whole spices (cinnamon, cloves, cardamom)
  • Fresh coriander
  • Fresh mint
  • Lemon
  • Cooking oil or ghee

Steps:

  1. Wash and soak rice for 30 minutes.
  2. Fry onions until golden brown and set aside half for garnish.
  3. Cook beef with onions, tomatoes, yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, and spices until tender.
  4. Boil rice with whole spices until 70% cooked, then drain.
  5. Layer cooked beef and rice in a pot.
  6. Add fried onions, coriander, mint, and lemon juice.
  7. Cover and cook on low heat (dum) for 20-25 minutes.
  8. Let it rest, then mix gently and serve hot with raita.
